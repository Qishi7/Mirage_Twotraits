# Background multiplier sensitivity

Fixed design: background_prob = 0.05, 500 genes, 15 variants/gene/trait, 3000 cases and 3000 controls per trait.

The boundary is around multiplier 4-5. Multiplier 4 is approximately tied with mTADA in the 10-rep coarse run; multiplier 5 is the lowest tested setting where MIRAGE is clearly better across all three targets and the high-background variant AF remains below 0.1%.
